Respected Evaluation Committee,

In response to Question 17 (Certificate of Standardization) of the Khmer Enterprise Startup Awards 2026 application, MAYANY Enterprise hereby submits this official declaration regarding our food safety and facility quality standardization roadmap.

1
Strategic Institutional Partnership We are actively collaborating in a technical assistance framework supported by Khmer Enterprise (KE) and executed in direct partnership with Control Union (Cambodia) Co., Ltd., a globally accredited certification body.
2
Scope of Facility Standardization The engagement includes comprehensive food safety gap analysis, hygienic process flow redesign, equipment specification alignment, and standard operating procedure (SOP) implementation for HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point) and GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice).
3
Target Certification Milestone Facility audits and physical upgrades are actively underway. Full compliance validation, final auditing, and official HACCP/GMP certification are scheduled for completion by Q4 2026 (December 2026).

This proactive standardization ensures that all MAYANY ready-to-mix cashew drink formulations, gourmet roasted cashew snacks, and franchise outlet F&B inputs adhere strictly to international food safety, traceability, and export quality standards.
