Slide 2 • The Problem
Market Inefficiencies & Regulatory Roadblocks

Why Cambodian Agro-MSMEs Lose $1B+ Annually

1

85%+ Exported Raw

Cambodia produces over 1,000,000 MT of raw cashew nuts (RCN), but lack of traceable supply chains forces farmers to sell raw crop to middlemen at raw commodity discounts without local value addition.

2

80% Rejection Rate

Value-added food MSMEs (cashew butter, roasted nuts, dried fruits) struggle with National Standard CS 001:2000 labeling, barcode costs, and mandatory nutrition tables, leading to rejection from modern supermarkets.

3

EU & Export Compliance

New international laws (such as the EU Deforestation Regulation - EUDR) require precise farm GPS polygons and batch traceability that traditional paper notebooks cannot satisfy.

Core Barrier: Existing enterprise ERP systems are too expensive and complex for smallholders. Opportunity: Lightweight AI-Native SaaS
